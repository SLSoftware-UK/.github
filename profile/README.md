## Tinkari

Our flagship product. [Tinkari](https://tinkari.com) is a multi-tenant SaaS platform for small workshops, electronics technicians, and tradespeople — a single sign-on across a growing suite of apps:

- **CRM** — customers, companies, and job pipelines
- **Inventory** — stock control, barcodes, and bills of materials
- **Scan** — a handheld warehouse companion for Inventory
- **Bench** — repair-job tracking with AI-assisted notes
- **Comms** — branded email, shared across every app above

Every app shares one login and one set of customer data — nothing is duplicated between them.
